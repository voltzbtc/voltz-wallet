## Country flags

The 257 SVG files in `src/assets/flags/` come from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons)
(the `flags/4x3` set), under the MIT License. Six of them (`bz`, `fk`, `gs`, `gt`, `mx`, `ni`) are
edited to inline their gradient stops, because react-native-svg does not follow a gradient's
`xlink:href`.

Copyright (c) 2013 Panayiotis Lipiridis

## Network and coin marks

Fifteen SVG files in `src/assets/stable-icons/` come from
[web3icons](https://github.com/0xa3k5/web3icons) (the `background` variant of the `@web3icons/core`
package), under the MIT License, with the package's `class` attribute removed. They are `arbitrum`,
`arc`, `avalanche`, `bsc`, `ethereum`, `hyperliquid`, `monad`, `optimism`, `plasma`, `polygon`,
`solana`, `tempo`, `tron`, `usdc` and `usdt`.

Copyright (c) 2024 0xa3k5

`src/assets/stable-icons/base.svg` is drawn in this repository after Base's own mark, a white square
on blue. No licence applies beyond the trademark statement above.

## Spark mark

`src/assets/stable-icons/spark.svg` is the Spark asterisk, the light version from the Spark brand
assets at <https://www.spark.money/brand>. Its shape is unaltered, scaled onto a dark square with its
clear space, as the brand guidelines on that page ask. It is used under those guidelines, not
under the MIT License, and no separate licence is published with the assets.

## Bitcoin mark

`src/assets/asset-icons/bitcoin.svg` is the Bitcoin symbol, which is in the public domain (see
<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bitcoin.svg>). The file matches the vector published at
<https://cryptologos.cc/logos/bitcoin-btc-logo.svg>, with the orange changed to the app's primary
colour. That site publishes no licence for its files.

## USDB mark

`src/assets/asset-icons/usdb.svg` is the USDB token mark, the file that the Flashnet token list
(<https://flashnet.xyz/api/tokenlist>) names as the USDB logo, with only its width and height
changed. No licence is published with it, so its terms are unconfirmed.

## DePix mark

`src/assets/asset-icons/depix.svg` is the DePix mark of Eulen, shown only to name the DePix token.
Its exact source is unconfirmed and no licence is on record for it.
